# Predator Abilities

The following options are available for Fishmen who are predator-type fish such as sharks, barracudas, etc. 

### Biting Parry

**10 points**

_Prerequisite:_ Sharp Teeth

Your strengthened jaw and teeth allow you to use your bite in combat more effectively. This allows you to use your Sharp Teeth to parry attacks. When parrying with your bite, you chomp down on the weapon, dealing your full bite damage to the parried weapon (or the attacker’s limb if parrying an unarmed attack). If you fail your parry, the attacker has the option to redirect their target to your jaw without any penalty.

Additionally, your strengthened jaw gives +2 DR to your jaw and whenever your bite attack is parried, you may apply your full bite damage to the defender’s weapon. 

# Pufferfish Abilities

The following options are available for Fishmen who are a type of pufferfish or similar fish.

### Improved Puff

**25 Points**

_Prerequisite:_ Puff, ST 15+

You puff up faster, dealing more damage and knockback. This increases both the impaling and knockback damage to 2d+1. This advantage also allows you to buy the below Puff Enhancement advantages.

### Puff Cushion

**9 points**

_Prerequisite:_ Improved Puff

You can puff up to reduce fall damage. You must have enough space to fully puff up (based on the Area of Effect of your Puff attack). This is equivalent to the Catfall advantage ([p. B41](B41)), though with an Accessibility (Only with enough space to puff up) limitation.

### Puff Bounce

**14 points**

_Prerequisite:_ Improved Puff, Puff Cushion

You are skilled at using your bouncy puffed-up form to move around. This is equivalent to the Super Jump ([p. B89](B89))  advantage at level 1 with the Bouncing enhancement and Full Power Only limitation ([GURPS Powers p. 80](P80)).

## Pufferfish Perks

### Aggressive Cushion

_Prerequisite:_ Puff Cushion

You gain the ability to use your Puff Cushion and Puff Attack at the same time. This allows you to attempt to break-free from any bounds or open up enough space to stop your fall per Puff Bounce.
